What are Choripinchos?
CHORIZO COLOMBIANO · ESTILO PINCHO

All the chorizo. None of the casing.

Choripinchos are Colombian-inspired, kabob-style chorizos: the familiar seasoned beef mixture shaped around a wide skewer and grilled over charcoal. No stuffing, no casings—just juicy centers, crisp edges, and that smoky flavor from the coals.

Para papá: no estamos cambiando el sabor del chorizo. Solo cambiamos la manera de prepararlo. La misma mezcla, directa a las brasas.

THE FIELD GUIDE

The Choripincho method

The pitch to papá

The traditional chorizo mixture stays the star. Instead of filling a casing, you press the seasoned meat onto a wide, flat skewer and grill it over coals. The goal: chorizo flavor, caramelized ridges, and a juicy interior—served hot with arepa and lime.

Cooking workflow

  1. Use chilled, trimmed lean beef and raw beef fat. Keep everything cold while chopping and mixing.
  2. Mix in salt until the ground portion becomes tacky, then fold in the cold diced meat, fat, aromatics and seasonings. Use the 75/25 ratio as a starting experiment.
  3. Refrigerate promptly. Raw ground beef should be cooked or frozen within 1–2 days total.
  4. Press firmly around clean, wide flat skewers. Chill shaped pinchos before grilling to improve hold.
  5. Cook over charcoal, turning carefully, until the center reaches 160°F (71°C). Serve with arepa and lime.
Food safety: this is a fresh beef sausage-style mixture, not a validated fermented or dry-cured recipe. Never air-dry raw mixture at room temperature. If testing acid ingredients, use only food-safe products and the manufacturer's directions. Acid does not replace proper refrigeration or thorough cooking.

A different technique, not a replacement

Traditional chorizo

Stuff into casings; fry, grill or otherwise cook. Familiar shape and preparation ritual.

Choripinchos

Press directly onto wide skewers. More exposed surface to char, no stuffing equipment.
